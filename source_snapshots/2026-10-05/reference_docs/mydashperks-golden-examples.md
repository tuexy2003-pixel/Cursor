## GE-001 — Target cart + Dad grocery money + “not stinky” overlay

**Intake note (owner):** AI-generated deal screenshot family. Future GPT/image flow should do *similar structure*, but the discount line should read as a **mydashperks.com discount** (named perk), not a generic “Extra $X off.” Teaching example only — not a remake request.

**SOURCE SETUP**  
Viewer lands on what looks like a real Target Secure Checkout: iPad (huge red discount) mixed with body wash, deodorant, laundry pods; dad text at top (“I sent $180 for groceries”); center overlay “$40 just to not be stinky.”

**HUMAN REASON TO CARE**  
Relatable inflation vent + curiosity about the “too good” iPad price + irony that hygiene costs feel as painful as the “steal.” People stop to check if the math / deal is real.

**INFORMATION CHANGE**  
Single-frame dense reveal: notification (money + purpose) → cart contents (want + needs) → overlay reframes the story around everyday essentials while the big discount sits in plain sight.

**PAYOFF**  
Discovering the mismatch: grocery money / “stinky” complaint vs. an electronics steal. Reward is the “wait… look at the iPad line” double-take.

**INSPECTABLE DETAIL**  
Dad $180 notice; iPad $449.99 with −$380; Dove / Native / Cleverly prices; delivery fee; total; overlay; TikTok search chip “ipad target.” (Math/UI oddities signal AI mock.)

**TRANSFERABLE MECHANIC**  
**Constraint money + mixed cart + red-herring vent overlay + named deep discount on the want item.** Viewer is invited to *inspect* the receipt, not be sold a pitch.

**WHAT MUST NOT BE COPIED**  
Exact Dad text; “$40 just to not be stinky”; specific Target cart SKUs/layout; creator face/handle; fake glitch math as a brand promise; presenting AI UI as a real Target glitch.

**POSSIBLE MYDASHPERKS CONNECTION**  
Natural: replace anonymous “Extra $380 off” with a visible **mydashperks.com … off applied** line on the same receipt style, while the plot stays inflation / cart inspection — perk is secondary proof, not the rant.

**WOULD IT STILL WORK WITHOUT MYDASHPERKS?**  
YES

**MUTATION POTENTIAL**  
1. Roommate Venmo for “shared household stuff” + cart that hides a big perk on a want.  
2. “Only buying toiletries” story + delivery app checkout where the perk line is what makes the total make sense.  
3. Parent “gas money” text + grocery+snack cart where the inspectable win is the named discount, not electronics.
