# VIRAL CREATIVE EXAMPLE CARDS

RESEARCH-MODE analysis. Built Mon 2026-10-05 ET. Inventory IDs refer to `VIRAL_CREATIVE_CORPUS_INVENTORY.md`.

## Comment-evidence labels (kept separate on every card)
- **CAPTURED ORGANIC:** text and like counts copied from `outputs/viral_comments_all_20261003.txt`, `outputs/account_review_20261002.md`, `outputs/tut_pinned_comments_20261004.txt`, or an inspected comment screenshot.
- **CAPTURED SUSPECTED-SEEDED:** captured, but the pattern suggests a plant: 0–2 likes, "tried it, worked tysm", handle plugs.
- **SYNTHETIC (predicted):** the clusters I *predict* a post would draw. These are labeled predictions, NOT evidence. Used only where nothing was captured.

**"Staged" note:** per the owner's correction, P01, P02 and the other own posts were STAGED/fictional. Their success is evidence that staged stories work when the mechanics are right.

## Field legend
- **Family:** STORY / DESIRE-UTILITY / HYBRID / OTHER.
- **Engines:** abstract mechanic codes, defined in `VIRAL_STORY_MECHANIC_TAXONOMY.md`.
- **Psych:** pattern codes, defined in `VIRAL_PSYCHOLOGY_PATTERN_BANK.md`.
- **Object role:** what the product does in the story. **Replaceability:** what happens if the object is swapped for another of similar desire.
- **Info state:** who knows what, at each beat.
- **J / M / A / R:** does the post invite JUDGMENT, METHOD question, AUDIT (checking the numbers), REPLICATION ("I'm doing this")?
- **Commercial position:** where the mydashperks line sits and whether the story *needs* it.

### CARD P01: "soo I overslept my DoorDash delivery 😭" (pink iPad), @hauls_bybrooke. WIN.
- **Status/perf:** POSTED Sep 17. 2.32M plays / 162K likes / 655 cmts / 1,482 shares / 3,159 saves. STAGED.
- **Family:** HYBRID, story-led. The story stands without the discount; the discount is a second engine the viewer *discovers*.
- **Hook:** overlay on slide 1, "soo I overslept my DoorDash delivery 😭". Hook type: confessed personal mistake + an outcome to find out (a mishap with an unknown result).
- **Engines:**
  - Primary: E-MISHAP-OUTCOME (narrator error → what happened to the thing?) + E-KINDNESS-REVEAL (stranger behaves better than expected).
  - Secondary: E-BURIED-NUMBER (an anomalous price sits in the frame unremarked).
  - Tertiary: E-JUDGMENT-SPLIT (narrator negligence vs dasher heroics).
- **Object role:** the stakes amplifier. An expensive item left outside raises the danger of the mishap.
  - **Replaceability:** HIGH for the story (any high-value, theft-attractive item works).
  - LOW for the *exact* reaction: the pink colour and "whole iPad" carried the absurdity ("YOU CAN DOORDASH A WHOLE ASS IPAD" 940).
  - P05 swapped in a MacBook with the same kit and did not repeat the result, so the object alone is NOT the engine (see contrast C1).
- **Info state:**
  - Slide 1: viewer knows there was a mishap and that something expensive was ordered. Does not know the outcome. Can SEE the price/discount but has not been told about it.
  - Slide 2: the dasher's text reveals the kindness and the hiding spot. Viewer now knows more than "sleeping narrator".
  - Slide 3: photo proof of the hiding spot.
- **Open loop:** "Where's the iPad / was it stolen?" Closed by slide 2–3. A second loop, "wait, $449 → $90 how?", is **never closed by any character**. The viewer must open it themselves, which drives comments and saves.
- **Escalation:** mishap → stranger's message (moral surprise) → physical proof. Short: 3 beats.
- **Proof:** a native order screen with the delivery-photo thumbnail, then a real-looking photo of the bag behind the AC. The proof belongs to the story (it answers "where is it?"), not to the ad.
- **Payoff:** relief + kindness. Viewer emotion: warmth ("very nice of them" 96.4K) and gentle mockery of the narrator.
- **J / M / A / R:** J = YES (narrator's carelessness; dasher praise). M = YES, latent (discount; 22.8K "why is no one talking about the discount"). A = YES ("$449 to $90 is crazier than the actual story" 6.9K; tip debates). R = weak/indirect.
- **Share energy:** "look how nice this dasher was" + "look at this price". Two different share reasons for two audiences.
- **Commercial position:** the mydashperks line sits as a native receipt row on slide 1. It is never mentioned in the story. The story does not need it, and that is why it reads as found rather than sold.
- **Surface grammar:**
  - S1: DoorDash Target "Order Complete" screen with the delivery-photo thumbnail; overlay text = hook.
  - S2: dark-mode iMessage, all incoming messages from "Kasai", full chrome.
  - S3: real-looking camera photo of the bag behind the AC unit.
  - Zero ad typography.
- **Slide-by-slide:**
  1. Order complete. $449 iPad, mydashperks.com Discount −$389, $10 tip, total $90.29. Thumbnail already shows the bag by the AC (the proof is seeded on slide 1).
  2. iMessage from the dasher: "expensive item… hide it somewhere safe… check the delivery photo when you wake up!"
  3. Photo: Target bag tucked behind the AC.
- **Why native:**
  - First-person mishap; a stranger's voice, not the narrator's, carries the kindness.
  - The price is an unmentioned artifact.
  - Each surface is a real app state the narrator would actually screenshot.
  - The photo is mundane and imperfect.
- **Ad-like risk present:** discount rows visible on the hook slide. Some viewers flag it: "marketing scheme… seen five tiktoks like this" 40; "promo code" 8. These are a small minority.
- **Comments:**
  - CAPTURED ORGANIC:
    - "wait this is very nice of them" 96.4K
    - "how tf u fall asleep with a whole iPad on the way" 35.2K
    - "why is no one talking about the discount" 22.8K / 58 replies
    - "$449 to $90 is crazier than the actual story" 6.9K
    - tip comments 3.3K / 965
    - "YOU CAN DOORDASH A WHOLE ASS IPAD" 940
    - "marketing scheme… seen five tiktoks like this" 40
    - "promo code" 8
  - CAPTURED SUSPECTED-SEEDED: none identified.
  - Clusters: KINDNESS (largest), NARRATOR-MOCK, DISCOUNT-DISCOVERY, PRICE-AUDIT, TIP-FAIRNESS, ABSURDITY, AD-SUSPICION (small).
- **Transferable mechanic:**
  - A narrator error puts a valuable thing at risk.
  - A third party's unexpected decency resolves it.
  - Proof photo.
  - An anomalous price sits unmentioned in the receipt so viewers "discover" it.
  - The viewer, not a character, asks "how?".
- **Non-transferable (copy risk):** oversleeping a delivery; the dasher hiding the item; hiding behind an AC; a pink iPad; the dasher's phrasing; the exact $449/$389/$90 numbers; the 3-slide order→text→photo sequence with the same roles.
- **Copy-risk:** HIGH if any 3 of those appear together. P05 and P06 already burned the hide-the-bag kit.
- **Confidence:** H.
