# VIRAL PSYCHOLOGY PATTERN BANK

The viewer-psychology patterns behind the mechanics. Built Mon 2026-10-05 ET.

**How to read each pattern:**
- WHEN IT WORKS
- WHAT IT NEEDS
- WHAT KILLS IT
- FALSE POSITIVES (looks like the pattern but isn't)
- SUPPORTING EXAMPLES (inventory IDs + captured comment evidence)

Evidence tags:
- [C] = captured comment.
- [M] = metric.
- [A] = analytic inference only.

---
## PSY-01 JUDGMENT ("who's wrong?")
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** two parties each have a defensible position and the viewer has to supply the verdict. Best when the narrator is at least partly wrong.
- **NEEDS:**
  - a clear rule or expectation
  - a visible breach
  - both sides voiced, with real cadence
  - no character who "admits" fault
- **KILLS:**
  - one side is a strawman
  - the story pre-rules ("I know I was wrong")
  - the breach is trivial
  - the commerce line *is* the breach
- **FALSE POSITIVES:**
  - A tip fight you didn't intend (P03: the $0.00 tip pulled judgment onto the narrator, 866 / 661 replies [C]). That is judgment, but against the creative's goal.
  - Price outrage (P02 "strips are $30" [C]) is AUDIT, not judgment.
- **SUPPORTING:**
  - P02: "Why couldn't he cook?" 2.7K; "Both of yall wrong" 117 [C]; 11.8% like rate [M].
  - P01: "how tf u fall asleep with a whole iPad on the way" 35.2K [C].
  - R08–R10 [A]; X01 421.5K likes [M].

## PSY-02 OUTCOME DESIRE ("I need to see what happened")
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** the hook states an event with an unknown result and the reveal is ≥1 slide away.
- **NEEDS:** real stakes for the thing at risk; a withheld result; proof that resolves it.
- **KILLS:** showing the result on the hook slide; low stakes; a result that is boring once seen.
- **FALSE POSITIVE:** "part 2" captions with no unresolved question (P04 / P07).
- **SUPPORTING:** P01 (mishap → where is it?) [M]; R11 ("then she texted me this…") [A]; P03 (the gap revealed on the last slide) [M].

## PSY-03 OPEN QUESTION (a residual loop the post never closes)
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** after the story resolves, a *second* question remains that only the viewer can raise.
- **NEEDS:** the question must be visible but unmentioned (a buried number, an unexplained detail).
- **KILLS:** any character asking or answering it (P06, U01).
- **FALSE POSITIVE:** confusion. Viewers asking "what am I looking at?" is not engagement.
- **SUPPORTING:** P01: "why is no one talking about the discount" 22.8K / 58 replies [C].

## PSY-04 PROOF DEMAND ("prove it / is this real")
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** the claim is surprising, and the post supplies proof that belongs to the story.
- **NEEDS:** an imperfect, native proof (a photo, an order thumbnail, a screenshot with chrome).
- **KILLS:** proof that looks produced, or proof of the *deal* rather than the event (P06 receipt).
- **FALSE POSITIVE:** "promo code?" comments are method demand, not proof demand.
- **SUPPORTING:** P01 slide 3 photo [A]; P03 straw-in photo "with the straw in??! refund" 859 [C].

## PSY-05 METHOD GAP ("how did you do that?")
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** as a *secondary* reward, after a story or identity hook has done the main work.
- **NEEDS:** an anomaly the viewer notices themselves; no on-slide instructions.
- **KILLS:**
  - a character asking "how"
  - "read the receipt"
  - a caption saying "link in bio"
  - the method being the hook (that makes it an ad)
- **FALSE POSITIVES:**
  - Tut-pinned "tried it, worked tysm" threads. These are 0–2 likes and SUSPECTED SEEDED [C].
  - The high-like comments on those posts are about relationship and price, not method (TP002 "sold my Xbox…" 6,057 [C]).
- **SUPPORTING:** P01 22.8K [C]; P05 "How did you get that discount????" 54 [C] (method only, with no story heat, gave weak reach); donnam46z2 "where's the tutorial" 902 [C]; D-FD11/D-FD12 named-but-withheld method after abundance image [C].
- **FAMILY D NOTE:** named-but-withheld method is allowed as *secondary* when outcome+constraint already carry. Character answering the method on-slide remains banned.

## PSY-06 AUDIT (checking the numbers)
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** the numbers are visible and surprising, and viewers argue about them. This drives comments and shares.
- **NEEDS:** real-looking totals.
- **KILLS:**
  - Totals that don't add up. Note: production rules forbid invented totals anyway.
  - An audit that lands on "scam" because the net price is still high ("$40 off still $54 doordash scammy" 191 [C]).
- **FALSE POSITIVE:** an audit that reads as distrust of the platform. That helps reach, but may hurt conversion (the owner says P03 did not convert).
- **SUPPORTING:** P01 "$449 to $90 is crazier than the actual story" 6.9K [C]; P02 price comments [C]; TP002 "The basket was 12$" 3,080 [C]; D-FD05 macro audits [C]; D-FD07 "total: more than i can afford" 1,891 [C]; D-FD08 city strawberry-price audits 2,837 [C].

## PSY-07 REPLICATION ("I'm doing this")
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** desire/utility posts where the outcome is attainable and savable (saves ≫ comments).
- **NEEDS:** a clear, attainable outcome image; a relatable identity constraint OR a savable map/steps structure.
- **KILLS:** a modeled replication by a character ("gonna try it", P06), which reads as a testimonial.
- **FALSE POSITIVE:** seeded "worked for me" comments.
- **SUPPORTING:** TP002 21K saves [M]; P01 3.2K saves [M]; D-FD01 1.15M saves [M]; D-FD03 360K saves [M]; D-FD05 ORGANIC "i just made it 10/10" 1,840 [C]; D-FD10 saves 52K ≫ comments 39 [M].

## PSY-08 IDENTITY ("this is so me / tag the friend who")
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** a self-label (broke, foodie, twenties, eldest daughter, gym bro) is shown with a self-aware contradiction.
- **NEEDS:** a specific, recognisable behaviour; a self-deprecating voice.
- **KILLS:** an aspirational or brag tone with no self-mockery.
- **SUPPORTING:** R16, R12, R07 [A]; TP002 "broke but…" [M].

## PSY-09 SOCIAL-RULE VIOLATION
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** an unwritten rule is broken (reading someone's receipt, asking for gift money back, a repost as a message).
- **NEEDS:** the violation actually happens on-slide.
- **KILLS:** the hook promising a violation the story never delivers (P06 "nosy" → a friendly method chat).
- **SUPPORTING:** R10 [A]; R14 [A]; P06 as the counter-example [M: 0.45%].

## PSY-10 ABSURDITY
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** one detail is too specific or ridiculous to be invented-sounding (straw in; a single cent left; hidden behind an AC).
- **NEEDS:** one absurd detail, not several; delivered deadpan.
- **KILLS:** stacking jokes, or a meme overlay explaining the joke.
- **SUPPORTING:** P03 "with the straw in" 859 [C]; P01 "YOU CAN DOORDASH A WHOLE ASS IPAD" 940 [C]; R12 [A].

## PSY-11 FAIRNESS / RECIPROCITY
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** tips, splitting bills, who-paid-for-what, gift money.
- **NEEDS:** a clear ledger.
- **KILLS:** an *unintended* fairness reading against the narrator (a $0 tip in P03).
- **SUPPORTING:** P01 tip comments 3.3K / 965 [C]; P03 tipping 866 / 661 [C]; R08–R10 [A].

## PSY-12 KINDNESS / FAITH-IN-HUMANITY
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** an unexpected decency from someone with no obligation.
- **NEEDS:** a stranger or low-status voice; a small, concrete act.
- **KILLS:**
  - the kind act being self-interested
  - repetition of the same kind-stranger role (P05)
- **SUPPORTING:** P01 "wait this is very nice of them" 96.4K [C]. This is the single largest comment in the corpus.

## PSY-13 COMPLICITY (the viewer knows what a character doesn't)
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** the narrator shows the viewer the secret (the order) while the other party is in the dark.
- **NEEDS:** an information asymmetry that is visible on slide.
- **KILLS:** the other party finding out on the same post with no tension.
- **SUPPORTING:** P02 slide 2 [A].

## PSY-14 ENVY / FLEX (weak alone)
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** only combined with a gratitude, reaction or identity layer (R05, TP004).
- **KILLS:** alone it yields scroll-past (P07: 0 comments [M]).

## PSY-15 AD-SUSPICION (the negative pattern)
- **TRIGGERS:**
  - the site or discount is the narrative center
  - a character testimonial ("gonna try it")
  - receipt-first with no story
  - five similar posts seen ("marketing scheme… seen five tiktoks like this" 40 [C])
- **MITIGATION:**
  - the story stands without the discount
  - the number stays unmentioned
  - rotate mechanics, not just objects
- **SUPPORTING:** P03 "undisclosed scam ad" 6 [C]; P06 0.45% [M].

## PSY-16 SAVE-PRIMARY (Family D signal)
- **WHEN IT WORKS:** viewers treat the post as a keepable artifact (map, recipe structure, board, checklist). Saves ≫ comments is the tell.

- **KILLS:** brand-forward listicles without craft (D-FW01–03); browse hauls (D-FW04).
