PURPOSE This is a structural/visual reference bank for building
staged/original Target-style commerce surfaces and selecting the correct
artifact for a storyboard.

CORE RULE Do not treat every image as one universal template. Choose the
reference family whose native information state actually serves the
story.

GLOBAL REFERENCE RULES 1. STORYBOARD FIRST. Pick the artifact after the
narrative information turn is known. 2. Preserve INFORMATION FUNCTION,
not a favorite screenshot. 3. Never combine unrelated Target states and
pretend they are one native screen. 4. Dates are semantic. Order placed,
scheduled, estimated arrival, delivered, picked up, return complete,
refund issued, and refund processed are different facts. 5. For exact
UI/text/numbers, deterministic reconstruction is preferred over asking
an image model to redraw an entire screenshot. 6. For product swaps, use
the supplied Target page as structural base and independently obtain the
target object’s current product image/title/price information. 7. For
physical photos, use references for composition/photographic language;
do not copy a stranger’s unique environment or identity. 8. Keep
staged/original commerce artifacts internally consistent across product,
price, quantity, dates, fulfillment state, totals, and later story
surfaces. 9. Do not let a reference force a story. If the storyboard
needs a fact no genuine reference supports, find a better artifact or
hold/rewrite that reveal. 10. Treat this bank as a starting set, not a
limit. Search for a new reference when the story requires a state absent
here.

BYOMA CHRONOLOGY NOTE The earlier concern that Target could not
naturally show item identity + acquisition chronology on one surface is
resolved structurally by references 13 and 15: they visibly show an
‘Order placed’ timestamp and the item on the same artifact. A future
BYOMA version should recreate that information function with the actual
BYOMA item and story dates; it should not reuse Nintendo facts or claim
the product medically caused a breakout.
