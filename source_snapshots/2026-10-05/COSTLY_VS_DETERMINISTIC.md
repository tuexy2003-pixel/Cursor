# MODEL-LED vs DETERMINISTIC
## Model / creative intelligence should do
- Premise creation; Family I vs D framing; gate judgments
- Human-natural dialogue; the "would a person text this?" judgment
- Hook quality; overlay choice (adds new context?)
- Propulsion diagnosis and smallest-causal-beat repair
- Viral texture ideation; story-native vs random detail judgment; comment-door prediction
- Judging contrivance and life-world fit (15–20)
- Visual judgment: does it look native or AI-perfect; route selection when ambiguous
- Image editing itself (image model)
- Creative interpretation of comments and performance
## Deterministic software should do
- Arithmetic (subtotal, discount, tax, total) and amount consistency across slides
- Date/weekday validation; status-bar time ordering across slides; ledger consistency
- STORY_LOCK versioning (immutable versions, diffs, approval stamps)
- Stale-asset detection (asset references a superseded lock version/field)
- Aspect/resolution validation (9:19.6 ≈ 0.4602; reject 9:16)
- Product-model, quantity and color continuity checks against the lock
- File provenance (base, ingredients, prompt, editor, parent asset, rights status)
- Rights flags (REFERENCE ONLY vs CLEARED) enforced at export
- Experiment tracking, performance ingestion, comment scraping/clustering storage
- Duplicate/mechanic-fatigue lookup; asset indexing; batch orchestration; approval-gate enforcement
