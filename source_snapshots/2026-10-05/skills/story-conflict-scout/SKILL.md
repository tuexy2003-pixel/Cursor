# Story & Conflict Scout

## When to use
Before Adaptation Blitz Match. This skill finds human material. It does not find the cultural object, and it does not write the carousel or the plot. Object & Culture Scout answers what people currently care about. Do not merge those jobs. Do not pair the top signal with the top object automatically.

## Event-first order
Find specific existing human behaviors, events, or situations before any concept exists. Do not originate a story by combining neighboring true facts. People avoiding one thing, plus people buying another thing, is not evidence that people do both as one chain.

A qualifying event needs evidence that people are actually doing or reacting to substantially the same chain.

For each finding record: ACTUAL EVENT, WHO DID WHAT, WHY, WHAT HAPPENED NEXT, WHAT PEOPLE REACTED TO, SOURCE EVIDENCE. Then classify it.

DIRECT EVENT means substantially the complete behavior exists in the evidence. REPEATED PATTERN means multiple examples establish the same behavior even when details differ. INGREDIENT ONLY means a useful fact or behavior that is not a complete event. Do not turn an ingredient-only finding into a story during this pass.

Only after that classification, and only for a direct event or a strong repeated pattern, notice whether the event already contains an object or purchase, a transaction artifact, a human loop, replication or desire, observed economic curiosity, and clean campaign space. Do not modify the event to create those. Do not search for a discount that fits. Do not search for MyDashPerks.

Mine the mechanic. Do not copy names, dialogue, exact amounts, jokes, distinctive circumstances, or the exact visual sequence. The transferable shape is source event, then why people care, then the abstract mechanic. An original execution comes later, and only from mechanics that passed this test.

## Two outputs
A run may return either or both. Neither is preferred. During an event-first pass, return event findings, not concepts.

## Mode A, found story
A real situation that already has a human tension or a reaction pattern: a recognizable complaint, a behavior people are arguing about, a customer situation, a social-norm conflict, a mistake and its consequence, or a retailer and customer incident.

A source proves the situation creates reactions. It is not a script. Never carry identifying details, usernames, exact dialogue, exact dollar amounts, distinctive jokes, distinctive circumstances, or somebody else's complete post.

For each found story keep: SOURCE SITUATION, HUMAN TENSION, ARGUMENT TYPE, STORY MECHANIC, WHY PEOPLE REACTED, TRANSFERABLE VARIABLES, NON-TRANSFERABLE DETAILS, OBJECT DEPENDENCE, NATURAL TRANSACTION ARTIFACT or none, COMMERCIAL QUESTION or none, CURRENT EVIDENCE, CONFIDENCE, and the evidence class above.

## Mode B, story ingredients
Research does not need a finished plot. One source does not need a beginning, middle, and end, and one source does not need to prove every fact. Behavior may be established at the category level. It does not need a discussion of the exact listing.

A category behavior and a separately verified object in that category may both be ingredients. They stay ingredients until a later step is allowed to combine them. An event-first pass does not combine them.

Collect reusable pieces, and keep a record even when some fields are unresolved:

- OBJECT OR CATEGORY
- DESIRE OR RECOGNITION
- HUMAN BEHAVIOR
- EXPECTATION OR SOCIAL NORM
- FRICTION, MISUNDERSTANDING, OR DECISION
- PURCHASE BEHAVIOR
- NATURAL TRANSACTION ARTIFACT, or none
- PRICE STAKE: exact, indexed, approximate, or unknown
- CURRENTNESS: current signal, persistent behavior, or stale and weak
- SOURCE PROVENANCE BY FACT
- UNRESOLVED FIELDS
- EVIDENCE CLASS: ingredient only, unless the same source is also a direct event or a repeated pattern

Do not discard the record because the price stake is unknown. Never invent a number, and do not turn an approximate price into an exact retailer price. Exact cents are not required at this stage. If a later reaction depends on the price, mark that dependency so it can be hardened before Gate 1. Otherwise price verification waits until a concept survives.

Also useful when a source actually shows them: gifting or household behavior, a third-party decision, language people use, an object-specific quirk, and an ordinary consequence.

A thread, a review, a complaint, a trend, or a product discussion may show that a behavior exists, that an object is wanted, that people react, that an expectation exists, or that a price has stakes. That is enough. Several weak signals may inform one later situation only after an event-first pass has earned that combination. Do not turn the thread into the concept.

Label the record GROUNDED INGREDIENT, not a plot. Also keep WHAT MUST NOT BE COPIED and CONFIDENCE.

## How to search
Breadth before depth. Look across relationships, friends, family, gifting, shopping, money, mistakes, work, and ordinary misunderstandings, and across more than one environment. Several subreddits inside one community do not count. Conflict is not only anger. Also generosity, embarrassment, regret, temptation, expectation versus reality, secrecy, and fairness.

Search behavior as soon as a category or object world looks promising. Do not wait for an exact product page. Ask how people use it, what goes wrong, what they misunderstand, argue about, regret, return, substitute, gift, share, hide, or compare, and what expectation surrounds it. Search colloquially. Also search advice, confession, am-I-wrong, purchase regret, delivery, and replication threads when the pass is event-first. Do not limit the search to one retailer.

Reddit and similar sources are for behavior, language, opinions, complaints, expectations, desire, and lived experience. They are not a screenplay database, and they are not required. If a direct community page will not open, that is not proof the behavior does not exist. Use another accessible review, forum, social, search, editorial, or comment source.

Current does not mean newsworthy. A normal product people still buy can carry a useful behavior, and a behavior does not have to have started today. Do not attach stale evidence only because nothing newer opened. A durable ordinary behavior may stay usable when the source is older. A temporary trend needs a current signal.

A bind or a desire can point at an outcome, then at whether access or economics are already part of it. That is a direction, not a formula. Do not invent a bind around a product so it will convert. An unverified price is unknown, not evidence. Do not drop a find because it has no transaction. Say none.

COMMERCIAL QUESTION is recorded only when a found situation already leaves one. If it does not, write none.

## Facts later
This relaxes early completeness only. It does not authorize fabrication. Unknown stays unknown until verified. Before production, any factual detail actually shown or relied upon gets the appropriate verification. If a surviving concept depends on a fact that later fails, revise or reject the concept.

Then stop. Do not write slides and do not synthesize the premise. Hand the classified findings to Adaptation Blitz only when a later step asks for a match.
