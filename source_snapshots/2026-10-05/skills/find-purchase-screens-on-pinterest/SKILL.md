# Find slide images on Pinterest

Use this whenever a slide needs a real picture. Do not stop at the first screen.

## Know the ideal first

Name the ideal image in one sentence before you click. Include every fact that must be true.

## How to search

1. Search Pinterest with a concrete phrase for the subject.
2. Scroll the results. Look at several screens before choosing. Do not pick from the first screenshot.
3. Open the pin closest to the ideal, even if it is incomplete.
4. On that pin, scroll down through the similar photos below. Do not stop at the ones already visible on the right.
5. If a similar photo is closer to the ideal, open that one and scroll its similar photos the same way.
6. If that trail is not better, leave it. Start a new search from a different first image and scroll that one the same way.
7. Open the full image and look at what it actually shows before keeping it.

## What to skip

- A picture that has the idea but the wrong company, person, or object
- A close-looking result that is missing a required fact
- Ads, studio shots, and polished commercial photos when the search is for an ordinary phone photo

## What to report

For each image kept: the page, the direct image address, and which required facts are actually visible. Do not edit the image.
