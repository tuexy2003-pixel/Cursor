# Blind review rubric

This rubric is for the human reviewer only. It is not part of a provider packet. Do not paste it into the model conversation that receives a packet.

Score each item 0, 1, or 2.

- 0: missing or contradicted
- 1: present but thin, generic, or partly off
- 2: specific and consistent with the packet

Leave a short note under each score. Do not treat a high score as permission to change the stored StoryLock or to create a Creative.

## Story-development audit

The subject is the current Target story. The model should diagnose it. It should not rewrite the story to be different.

| Item | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Preserves good decisions | Keeps the working hook, dialogue, line items, and continuity unless a real weakness is named |
| Story before commerce | Talks about the human situation before the basket |
| Human causality | Names what the person does, why the next beat follows, and what is at stake |
| Specific stakes | Stakes are concrete, not "engagement" or "relatability" |
| Comment doors | More than one door, tied to something already in the story |
| Viral texture | Names a texture that is already in the lock, or a missing one, without inventing a new ad |
| Secondary inspection | Notices a detail a second look would reward |
| Commerce integration | Commerce follows the story. It is not an ad that then grows a story |
| Proof boundary | Does not claim a screen proves intent, payment, location, or a promotion it does not show |
| Continuity | Uses the stored continuity instead of inventing a new timeline |
| Retailer language | Does not force retailer slogans or category labels the story does not use |
| Basket padding | Does not add expensive items to make the total look bigger |
| Production-ready judgment | READY, THIN, or NEEDS_CHANGES matches the diagnosis, and recommended changes are few |

## Concept generation

These concepts are proposals. They are not StoryLocks and they are not the Target creative.

| Item | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Account fit | Fits Maria's approved lane and relationships that are actually in the DNA |
| Age world | Behavior fits the stated life-world instead of a generic shopper |
| Novelty | New premises. Not a retelling of a named benchmark, and not the holdout Target story |
| Story-native commerce | The purchase falls out of the event |
| Comment doors | More than one plausible door per concept, when the concept is developed enough to have them |
| Viral texture | Opportunities are specific to the premise |
| Research flags | Missing facts are marked as research needed. The model does not pretend it searched the web |
| Economics | Does not pad a basket. Unknown prices stay unknown |
| Proof surface | Says what a later screen would need to show, without inventing proof |

## Notes

Record uncertainties. A concept field left null is acceptable when the packet does not contain that fact. A confident invention is not.
